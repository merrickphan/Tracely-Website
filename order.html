Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — subscriptions are month to month. Cancel in one click and keep access until the end of the billing period.

What counts as a check?

Tracely re-checks only sentences that changed, so a whole essay session usually costs just a handful of checks. Starter is generous; Student and Pro are unlimited.

Do I need a .edu email for Student?

No — the Student plan is for everyone. It's priced for students, not gated by them.
